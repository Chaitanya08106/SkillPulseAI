Information You Provide

  • Account Data: Name, email, phone, target role, experience level, location
  • Resume Content: Processed temporarily by AI for analysis; raw file not permanently stored
  • Interview Responses: Written answers stored securely for scoring and feedback
  • Profile Information: Any optional data you add to your profile

Automatically Collected Data

  • Device information (browser type, OS, screen resolution)
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used, session duration)
  • IP address (security and fraud prevention only)
  • Session cookies (authentication only, no cross-site tracking)

Resume Data Handling

Your resume is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI API to generate ATS scores and extract profile data. The raw resume content is not permanently stored — only analysis results (score, keywords, extracted fields) are saved to your profile.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the SkillPulse platform
  • To process and analyze your resume using AI systems
  • To generate personalized career recommendations and skill gap analysis
  • To facilitate connections between candidates and companies (consent required)
  • To send transactional emails (account verification, HR status updates)
  • To send marketing communications (only if you opted in during signup)
  • To detect and prevent fraud and security incidents

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell or rent your data. We may share with:

  • Companies (explicit consent): When you click "Send Resume," we share your name, email, role, and ATS score with that company's HR team only
  • Service Providers: Supabase (database), Anthropic (AI) — all bound by data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority

Data Retention

  • Account and profile data: retained while account is active
  • Interview responses: retained for 12 months
  • Raw resume files: deleted immediately after AI processing
